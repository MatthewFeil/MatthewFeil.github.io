This page explains optional usage analytics on matthewfeil.com. Your choice never affects access to the site's public tools.

## Your choice

Google Analytics loads only after you select **Yes** in the analytics notice. Selecting **No**, or leaving the notice unanswered, keeps it off. Your choice is saved in your browser for 180 days, after which the site asks again. If browser storage is unavailable, your choice lasts only for the current page.

Use **Analytics preferences** in the footer to change your choice. In Transcribe, the control is in the shortcuts sidebar; in Chicago 'L' Live Art, it is at the bottom of the screen. Withdrawal stops further collection and deletes this site's accessible Google Analytics cookies. It does not delete information already collected by Google.

The site also honors browser Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals by keeping analytics off.

## What analytics collects

With your permission, Google Analytics 4 measures page visits, basic engagement, deep scrolling, and clicks on external links. Information may include the page path, referring page, external link URL, browser and device details, approximate location, and timestamps. Page and referrer URLs sent by the site's initial configuration omit query strings and fragments.

Reports help me understand which pages people use and where the site could improve. I do not send your name, email address, local audio, uploaded filenames, calculator inputs, or private tracker contents to analytics. The Personal Space dashboard, portfolio and lifting trackers, Birds page, and this privacy page do not load the Google Analytics tag, even if you have accepted analytics elsewhere.

**The data is not fully anonymous.** Google Analytics uses cookies with pseudonymous identifiers to distinguish browsers and sessions. Reports commonly show grouped statistics, but the underlying identifiers can link visits from the same browser. This site does not use advertising personalization or Google Signals in its analytics tag.

## Cookies and retention

After acceptance, Google Analytics can set first-party cookies such as `_ga` and `_ga_DSJ82YWBSZ`. The site's tag limits their lifetime to 180 days without automatically extending it on each visit. The consent preference itself is stored locally in your browser.

Google processes the analytics data. User-level and event-level retention is controlled by the GA4 property's settings; standard GA4 supports two or fourteen months. Aggregated standard reports can remain available longer. See [Google's retention explanation](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/7667196?hl=en).

For more information, see [how Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites), [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), and [Google Analytics cookie information](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/11397207?hl=en). Google and its service providers may process information internationally.

## Other site services

This analytics choice controls Google Analytics. It does not disable the site's necessary hosting, saved display preferences, or services you choose to use. Transcribe processes audio locally in your browser. Signing in to Personal Space uses authentication and the backend services needed for its dashboard and trackers. External links and embedded services have their own privacy practices.
