## Design Specificity Verdict

The home page is a specific, authored personal portfolio: its oversized name treatment, red accent, and mix of transit art, calculators, music, and writing are not category-interchangeable. Its main weakness is direction, not identity: the hero operates as a second site map and presents six equal choices before showing which work matters most.

## What's Working

- The black, white, and red editorial system, square edges, and display hierarchy are cohesive across header, hero, and posts.
- The sequence of name, context, and recent work is logical; post dates and summaries make the site feel current.
- The mobile layout remains legible and ordered without overlapping content.

## Priority Issues

### [P1] The hero is a second, overfull navigation system

The header already provides site navigation. Repeating About and Posts beside four unrelated links makes every destination seem equal, and fills nearly the first mobile screen. Reserve the header for navigation; feature one primary project and at most two secondary work destinations in the hero. Suggested command: `/impeccable distill`.

### [P1] The hero lacks a clear start-here story

The name establishes personality but does not say why a first-time visitor should choose a project. Add one concise factual line describing projects, tools, and experiments, then give the representative current work a principal action. Suggested command: `/impeccable clarify`.

### [P2] Tools and posts are named too similarly

Investment Calculator in the hero, Stock Return Calculator in Tools, and Investment Calculators in posts require trial-and-error. Apply visible type labels such as Tool or Article. Suggested command: `/impeccable clarify`.

### [P2] The small-screen hero delays substantive content

At 390px, the name and six stacked links dominate the visible screen. Reduce the mobile hero minimum height and number or scale of hero links so the next section is hinted below the fold. Remove the measured 2px horizontal overflow. Suggested command: `/impeccable adapt`.

### [P3] Navigation animation favors layout changes

The overlay identified layout-property animation in the expanding header/dropdown behavior. Keep the reveal but prefer opacity and transform over padding or dimension changes where practical. Suggested command: `/impeccable optimize`.

## Persona Red Flags

- Jordan, a first-time visitor, sees a large name and six opaque choices, with no obvious first click.
- Alex, a goal-directed visitor, must distinguish several similarly named investment destinations across hero, navigation, and posts.
- Casey, a mobile visitor, sees mostly navigation in the first viewport rather than personal context or recent work.

## Minor Observations

- The overlay is visible in the fresh `[Human]` browser tab.
- The red first-letter treatment should support, not carry, hierarchy.
- Test the sticky header's `backdrop-filter` on lower-power mobile devices.
- The accessible Featured pages label is not visible to sighted users; a visible grouping label would improve scanning.

## Questions to Consider

What should the hero prioritize: the newest standout project, a compact project index, or the personal introduction? Should posts be as prominent as tools, or support the project work?
